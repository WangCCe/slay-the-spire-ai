## Why

Formal non-combat RL remains blocked on a credible non-teacher baseline floor
and source-comparable target-supported outcomes. The Current-policy simulator
bridge now has broader event and shop coverage, but both prior own-trajectory
cohorts stopped before one complete row and the new Courier support envelope
can intentionally censor episodes, so spending another untouched cohort before
fixing the baseline-floor evidence contract risks another non-informative run.

## What Changes

- Add a read-only, provenance-bound audit of the frozen readiness, policy,
  bridge, compatibility, shop-support, and outcome-feasibility evidence.
- Compare the remaining non-teacher baseline candidates and state explicitly
  whether Current is eligible for a later preregistration, structurally
  blocked, or not a viable floor candidate.
- Define the required treatment of unsupported or censored simulator episodes,
  comparison policies, absolute and paired gates, deterministic replay, stop
  rules, and untouched evaluation boundaries before any new seed is selected.
- Publish a compact machine-readable result and human-readable recommendation,
  then update project direction from the audited result.
- Keep all gameplay, native loading, fresh-cohort, model fitting, reward, OPE,
  formal-RL, training, qualification, loading, and promotion authority false.

Success means the same registered evidence deterministically yields one
reviewable go/no-go result and an exact next prerequisite without constructing
a simulator environment. No live evidence is created by this change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `noncombat-baseline-floor-readiness-audit`: Defines the immutable evidence,
  candidate comparison, unsupported-episode treatment, verdict, and no-authority
  contract for deciding whether a non-teacher baseline-floor study may be
  preregistered.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The change is limited to one compact offline audit implementation, its frozen
registration and reports, focused tests, OpenSpec artifacts, and
`docs/project_direction.md`. It does not modify gameplay policy, the simulator
adapter or bridge, rewards, models, external repositories, Communication Mod,
or prior evidence.

Rollback removes only the new audit artifacts and direction update. Historical
registrations, modules, consumed cohorts, local untracked evidence, and live
configuration remain byte-identical.
