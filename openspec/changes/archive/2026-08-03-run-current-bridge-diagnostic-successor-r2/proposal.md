## Why

The consumed Current bridge diagnostic failed before its first transition
because its runner required a field outside the production candidate contract.
The defect is fixed and regression-covered, but the completed-Current-row
structural blocker remains; the anti-retry review permits one new versioned
successor proposal while keeping the failed identity immutable.

## What Changes

- Add a narrow versioned diagnostic profile boundary so the consumed v1
  registration and artifacts remain exactly recomputable while one new r2
  registration and output identity can be validated without copying the full
  runner.
- Bind r2 to the immutable v1 registration, zero-row failure, canonical output,
  closeout, anti-retry decision, candidate-schema fix, exact current sources,
  native module, adapter/simulator provenance, metadata, runtime, and all-false
  authority.
- Preserve exactly seeds `7000`, `7100`, `2000`, and `10`, their order, two
  replays, 500 decisions per replay, 600 seconds, fixed verdict precedence, and
  the declared Courier support boundary. No fresh seed or runtime override is
  allowed.
- Require production-shaped pre-step regressions, dual-profile no-native
  verification, focused pytest, the partitioned commit gate, strict OpenSpec
  validation, and exact historical byte checks before r2 preregistration.
- Permit at most one native r2 execution only after its registration is
  committed and pushed and its output root is proven absent. Preserve any
  failure or partial journal as terminal and forbid r3 preparation in this
  change.
- Keep gameplay, fresh evidence, baseline-floor claims, readiness promotion,
  OPE, model fitting, reward changes, formal RL, training, qualification,
  policy/model loading, and promotion out of scope.

Success is either one independently recomputable structural result under the
unchanged reused-seed controls or one precise terminal failure. A pass permits
only a separate read-only baseline-floor readiness refresh. Before r2
publication, rollback may remove unconsumed r2 planning/implementation; after
the journal starts, rollback must preserve the new identity and all evidence.
