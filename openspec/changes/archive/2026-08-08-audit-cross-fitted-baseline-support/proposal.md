## Why

The independently verified r2 execution completed all 512 trajectories and
showed that cross-fitted attribution materially changes the policy gradient,
but 2,261 of 11,729 baseline predictions were clipped at zero and card-reward
`take` remained the greedy family for 1,773 of 1,774 final-window multi-family
decisions. Before proposing another algorithm or experiment, the sealed
evidence must distinguish baseline support failure from supported advantage
pressure and explain why exact saturation did not fire.

## What Changes

- Add a source-only audit that consumes only the tracked, independently
  verified r2 terminal bundle while holding its inactive execution lease.
- Revalidate the manifest, terminal identity, complete checkpoint/evidence
  chain, cross-fitted folds, baseline predictions, advantages, gradient ledger,
  and source identity before emitting analytical metrics.
- Stratify baseline clipping, residuals, advantages, card-reward family
  pressure, and greedy-family behavior by chunk, fold, category, selected
  family, prediction-support status, advantage sign, decision timing, and
  effective-floor band.
- Reconcile aggregate direct pressure with the recorded full gradient
  components and isolate the final-window 1,773/1 near-saturation exception.
- Publish byte-deterministic JSON and Markdown reports with a bounded
  descriptive verdict and all execution, training, evaluation, OPE, causal,
  gameplay, qualification, and promotion authority false.
- Success means two fresh source-only processes produce byte-identical reports
  from the unchanged sealed bundle and focused regressions prove fail-closed
  behavior. No favorable mechanism or policy result is required.
- If source, input, lease, arithmetic, support, determinism, or isolation checks
  fail, publish no analytical report and remove or revert only the additive
  uncommitted audit outputs; never modify the consumed r2 bundle.
