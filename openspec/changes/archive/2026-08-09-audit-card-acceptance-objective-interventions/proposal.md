## Why

The completed card-acceptance audit found positive direct `take` pressure in
all eight chunks while within-`take` concentration increased and direct
conditional margin pressure remained mixed. Before another empirical
successor, the repository needs a source-only comparison that determines
whether bounded objective interventions can protect conditional card choice
without treating the existing run as policy-quality or causal evidence.

## What Changes

- Add an immutable source-only audit over the exact published 20260809
  card-acceptance audit and its already bound r2 gradient evidence. It will
  verify source/report identities and reconstruct the five named gradient
  components without loading a model, native module, simulator, seed source,
  game, or CommunicationMod.
- Compare a fixed, non-tuned intervention set: the recorded full gradient; a
  structural card-reward family-policy ablation; and a conditional-conflict
  guard that projects only the conflicting portion of the card-reward family
  gradient away from the card-reward conditional gradient. These are
  descriptive counterfactuals, not selected algorithms or coefficients.
- Add pure synthetic score-space contracts for an independent acceptance
  coordinate. A registered nonzero multi-family acceptance change must leave
  within-`take` probabilities, ordering, entropy, and margins unchanged, while
  conditional changes must preserve the acceptance coordinate. One-family
  fallback keeps acceptance inactive and explicit.
- Report per-chunk and fixed early/final-window gradient norms, removals,
  projections, pairwise geometry, full-gradient displacement, and
  conditional-descent compatibility. Preserve zero-norm and non-conflict
  cases explicitly and publish no preferred intervention.
- Publish canonical JSON of at most 1 MiB and Markdown of at most 64 KiB from
  two fresh isolated source-only processes with byte-identical output, fixed
  verdict predicates, and an exact all-false authority map.
- Success requires exact reconstruction of all eight recorded chunk gradients,
  complete synthetic invariants, deterministic output, focused regressions,
  the configured commit/full phase gates, strict OpenSpec validation, and
  independent review.
- Non-goals are choosing or changing an objective, coefficient, optimizer,
  architecture, ranker, checkpoint, policy, reward, cohort, or test tier;
  fitting, training, replaying, evaluating, running OPE, qualifying, promoting,
  launching gameplay, or claiming policy quality or causal value.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `noncombat-card-acceptance-objective-intervention-audit`: Define immutable
  evidence binding, fixed gradient counterfactuals, independent-coordinate
  synthetic invariants, bounded compatibility classification, deterministic
  publication, and all-false downstream authority.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Adds one standard-library analysis module, one focused test file, one new
  capability specification, canonical dated JSON/Markdown reports, and one
  project-direction entry.
- Reuses only tracked reports and sealed r2 evidence already consumed by the
  prior audit. It does not mutate, copy, rediscover, or extend that cohort and
  adds no runtime dependency.
- The live evidence is the published verdict
  `acceptance_pressure_with_conditional_concentration_but_mixed_direct_pressure`
  over 3,536 card rewards, 512 trajectories, 11,729 decisions, and eight
  chunks; all existing training, evaluation, policy-quality, and causal
  authority remains false.
- The success metric is exact reconstruction of every bound gradient,
  invariant-preserving treatment of every fixed intervention including
  degenerate cases, and byte-identical isolated reports that select no
  downstream algorithm.
- Rollback removes only the new audit source, tests, reports, specification,
  archived change, and direction entry. Existing r2 artifacts, prior audits,
  checkpoints, policy code, test gates, and live configuration remain
  unchanged.
