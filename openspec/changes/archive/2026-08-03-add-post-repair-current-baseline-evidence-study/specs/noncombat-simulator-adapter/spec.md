## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reviewed Current Baseline Evidence Study
The adapter SHALL permit native simulator use by the post-repair Current
baseline-evidence study only under its exact accepted registration, fixed
cohorts, policies, conservative support contract, limits, and separate
execution authorization.

#### Scenario: Registered study constructs an environment
- **WHEN** the pushed registration, external provenance, seed exclusion,
  execution authorization, started journal, and absent output checks all pass
- **THEN** the adapter MAY construct only the registered A0 Ironclad Current and
  first-candidate environments in fixed cohort order
- **AND** it SHALL preserve baseline-controlled non-target behavior and the
  existing fail-closed shop and event support boundaries

#### Scenario: Study input exceeds the registered boundary
- **WHEN** a caller requests another seed, policy, ascension, module, support
  approximation, runtime override, retry, or environment outside the started
  attempt
- **THEN** the adapter SHALL reject the request before returning evidence
- **AND** it SHALL NOT substitute SimpleAgent, Bottled, or another candidate

#### Scenario: Registered study publishes a result
- **WHEN** canary stops or the conditional holdout completes under the exact
  adapter identity
- **THEN** simulator rows MAY support only the study's structural and
  baseline-floor classification
- **AND** live gameplay, target-supported outcomes, OPE, formal RL, training,
  loading, qualification, and promotion authority SHALL remain false
