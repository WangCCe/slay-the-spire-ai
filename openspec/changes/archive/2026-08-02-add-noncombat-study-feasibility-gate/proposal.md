## Why

The frozen 600-attempt outcome-evidence study requires at least three deterministic-Current-supported victories, but the only B3-B7 victory has target weight zero and no pre-launch analysis demonstrates that the fixed budget is likely to satisfy that gate. Eight qualification identities and substantial launch-governance work have produced no registered trajectory, so statistical feasibility must be reviewed before another replacement identity or approximately 24-hour collection is considered.

## What Changes

- Add a deterministic offline feasibility analyzer for a fixed attempt budget, required supported-victory count, observed complete trajectories, and observed target-supported victories.
- Preserve the analyzer, focused regression, and deterministic feasibility artifact bytes as LF across Windows checkouts.
- Report the exact binomial planning probability under the observed plug-in rate, the minimum supported-victory rate required to reach declared 50%, 80%, and 90% pass-probability levels, and a sensitivity table that does not present any scenario as a causal or guaranteed forecast.
- Keep raw behavior victories separate from target-supported victories; a zero-weight win cannot satisfy or estimate the registered supported-victory gate.
- Bind each audit to the exact registration and evidence artifact hashes and emit deterministic JSON and Markdown with an explicit `demonstrated` or `not_demonstrated` planning result.
- Require at least 100 source-qualified complete trajectories and an observed plug-in probability of at least 80% before statistical feasibility is demonstrated for an expensive fixed study.
- Apply the audit to the frozen B3-B7 evidence and current v2 registration. Until it passes or a separate change revises the evidence objective, keep replacement qualification, study `start`, gameplay collection, OPE reinterpretation, formal training, and policy promotion blocked.
- Preserve r1-r8, the v1 blocked study, all registrations, and every external artifact byte-for-byte. No game, Java, CommunicationMod, qualification, study, or training process is started.
- Success means reproducible calculations, focused tests, strict OpenSpec validation, a durable report, and a clear offline go/no-go. Rollback removes only this unarchived analyzer, tests, report, and planning artifacts.
