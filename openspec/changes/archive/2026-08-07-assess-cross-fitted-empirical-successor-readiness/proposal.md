## Why

The consumed cross-fitted execution completed only 11 of 12 debited accesses
and produced no complete chunk because repeated 63 MB registration validation
exhausted its fixed wall-time budget. The source-only control-plane repair now
passes its structural and repository gates, but synthetic correctness alone
does not prove that a new pushed identity, fresh cohort, and fixed resource
budget are ready for another irreversible empirical attempt.

## What Changes

- Add a source-only readiness auditor bound to the pushed repair source, the
  immutable failed terminal bundle, its bottleneck audit, and its repair
  closeout.
- Reconstruct the historical seed inventory from one exact Git tree, derive the
  canonical next 512-seed candidate schedule, and prove it is disjoint from the
  entire consumed 512-seed cohort rather than only the 12 debited seeds.
- Rehearse the actual-scale validated-context, journal/resource, terminal, and
  independent-verifier paths in an isolated temporary root without importing
  Torch, loading native code, constructing an environment, or treating seed
  integers as environment accesses.
- Claim the pushed source identity in a durable attempt journal before any
  gate runs. A claimed identity cannot be reused with different paths or an
  audit id; any later failure produces one typed, all-false terminal receipt.
- Pre-register a deterministic control-plane budget projection with a fixed
  safety multiplier and budget-share ceiling. Emit `go` only when structural
  validation counts, immutable source/evidence bindings, fresh-cohort
  disjointness, control-plane projection, and independent reconstruction all
  pass; otherwise emit one typed `no_go` reason.
- Publish the complete candidate inventory as deterministic gzip over canonical
  JSON, plus deterministic report JSON and Markdown, with every downstream
  authority false. Build and independently verify those artifacts in a hidden
  staging directory, then atomically expose the three-file publication only
  after verification succeeds. Stream actual-scale inventory encoding, Git
  reconstruction, and artifact comparison instead of duplicating the full
  inventory in memory.
- Supervise rehearsal and independent-verifier process trees with confirmed
  descendant termination and an immutable 900-second verifier ceiling. A `go`
  result makes only a later registration proposal eligible for review; it does
  not create a registration, request, approval, authorization, native load,
  seed access, fitting, training, evaluation, gameplay, formal-RL,
  qualification, or promotion authority.
- Keep estimator, folds, reward, feature projection, optimizer, family
  objective, schedule size, resource ceilings, and empirical success criteria
  unchanged. This is a readiness decision, not mechanism tuning.

Success means the auditor and an independent standard-library verifier produce
equivalent streamed decision inputs and one reproducible `go` publication or
typed `no_go` attempt receipt from the same pushed source and immutable
evidence. Rollback removes only the additive auditor, tests, attempt journal,
and report; consumed artifacts, checkpoints, game configuration, and seed
identities remain untouched.

## Capabilities

- `noncombat-cross-fitted-empirical-successor-readiness`: Define the source-only actual-scale, fresh-cohort, budget, independent-verification, and all-false-authority gate that must precede any new empirical successor registration.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Adds one analysis script, one independent standard-library verifier, focused
  tests, a source-identity attempt journal, and deterministic
  gzip/JSON/Markdown readiness evidence.
- Reads committed reports and Git blobs only; temporary rehearsal output must
  live under a caller-supplied isolated root and must be removed from the
  decision inventory after verification.
- Does not change production agent behavior, CommunicationMod configuration,
  native adapter/runtime semantics, checkpoints, policy parameters, rewards,
  or gameplay.
- Any later empirical successor remains a separate OpenSpec change with a new
  pushed source identity, fresh full registration, exact request, and separate
  explicit human approval.
