## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reviewed R8 Offline Replacement
The system SHALL prepare at most one previously absent r8 qualification candidate after the reviewed source-byte fix without changing any historical qualification evidence or live state before the offline go decision.

#### Scenario: R8 preparation starts from an absent identity
- **WHEN** the r8 root and every fixed claim, stage, request, handoff, result, failure, and attestation path are proven lexically absent
- **THEN** preparation SHALL freeze a clean source snapshot, the archived source-byte fix, exact runner and request inputs, the current CommunicationMod bytes, protected inventories, and zero-target-process observation
- **AND** it SHALL render one canonical bootstrap-v1/request-v3 no-action candidate with uniformly false study, gameplay, OPE, policy, causal, training, and promotion authority
- **AND** candidate bytes SHALL remain repository-local until the offline go/no-go gate passes

#### Scenario: R8 reuses prior verification evidence
- **WHEN** previously recorded focused or registered gate evidence is proposed as an r8 input
- **THEN** the offline review SHALL require exact equality of every bound executable, importable, and relevant test input hash
- **AND** candidate-specific focused checks, canonical replay, source-only review, strict OpenSpec validation, and diff checks SHALL still execute
- **AND** any input mismatch SHALL require the registered gate to rerun without substituting an unregistered raw full suite

#### Scenario: R8 offline review fails
- **WHEN** any identity, source, request, review, candidate, path, configuration, inventory, rollback, canonical-byte, hash, or authority check fails before publication
- **THEN** the external r8 root and CommunicationMod configuration SHALL remain unchanged and no Java, ModTheSpire, qualifier, child, or study process SHALL launch
- **AND** the candidate SHALL become obsolete and non-authorizing, and any implementation defect SHALL route to a separate regression-backed change without preparing r9

### Requirement: At-Most-Once R8 No-Action Qualification
The system SHALL publish and invoke r8 at most once after a current exact offline go decision and SHALL preserve an independently replayable terminal or fail-closed retirement.

#### Scenario: R8 is invoked through CommunicationMod
- **WHEN** the reviewed offline go record remains current, the exact external root and request-bound configuration are published, all source and live inventories still match, and no target process survives
- **THEN** the operator SHALL issue exactly one ModTheSpire invocation with CommunicationMod launching `D:\anaconda\envs\stsai\python.exe` through the reviewed launcher
- **AND** the qualifier SHALL own the no-action child lifecycle and no retry, second invocation, gameplay action, study `start`, trajectory collection, OPE, training, or policy change SHALL occur

#### Scenario: R8 reaches a complete valid terminal
- **WHEN** the bootstrap stages, active request, handoff, attempt, ready, release, child exit, restoration, terminal, external anchors, and independent verifier all validate
- **THEN** r8 SHALL be classified only as `qualified_for_start_review`
- **AND** configuration restoration, protected inventory equality, and zero surviving target processes SHALL be independently attested
- **AND** qualification SHALL grant no current study, gameplay, OPE, policy, causal, training, or promotion authority

#### Scenario: R8 is incomplete or uncertain
- **WHEN** publication, invocation, bootstrap, request, handoff, terminal, source, configuration, inventory, cleanup, verifier, or process evidence is absent, partial, invalid, failed, abrupt, or uncertain
- **THEN** the system SHALL classify only the exact evidence-supported obsolete or retired disposition, preserve every published root byte, restore controlled live state, and retire r8 permanently
- **AND** it SHALL NOT repair, retry, delete, relabel, or reuse r8 and SHALL NOT prepare r9 under this amendment

#### Scenario: R8 remains no-action
- **WHEN** any offline, publication, live, verification, or closeout branch executes
- **THEN** no registered study root, run lock, ledger, gameplay action, run record, AI marker, checkpoint, model, trajectory, OPE output, reward, policy, training artifact, or promotion authority SHALL be created or changed
