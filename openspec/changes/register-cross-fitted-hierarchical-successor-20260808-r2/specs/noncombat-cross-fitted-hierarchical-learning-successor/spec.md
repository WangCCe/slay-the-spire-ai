## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The 20260808-r2 registration is compact, exact, and all-false
The system SHALL publish at most one canonical compact v2 registration with
identity `noncombat-cross-fitted-hierarchical-learning-successor-20260808-r2`.
It SHALL bind repository source
`ffd9acc444258483d172529eccfe8ccb05c9bb9b`, readiness publication
`0c62aff2fb301dd491017bbf2e775c36a177bf67`, and readiness identity
`cdff8937e6c15b27df582bf8d9d606c2185d94cdcfbe4e4f907d6a0244e48d38`.
It SHALL reference the exact pushed r5 report, deterministic-gzip candidate,
and source-keyed independent-verification receipt; retain the complete ordered
eight-chunk, 512-seed schedule; and bind the exact source inventory, native
module and provenance, Windows CPU runtime, CommunicationMod configuration,
complete production-checkpoint tree, registered output root, controls, resource
ceilings, and lifecycle terms. Every authority value SHALL remain false.

#### Scenario: Exact source-only registration is published
- **WHEN** the pushed head descends from the exact readiness source and
  publication commits, all readiness evidence revalidates, every inert identity
  is reobserved, the 512-seed schedule agrees, and the registered output root is
  absent
- **THEN** the builder emits one canonical compact v2 registration and a
  deterministic review binding its bytes, digest, source/publication/readiness
  identities, producer validation, independent validation, all-false authority,
  and zero dependency-import or empirical-operation delta

#### Scenario: Any bound registration input drifts
- **WHEN** source ancestry, a readiness path or byte, readiness eligibility,
  candidate encoding or schedule, source inventory, native/runtime/isolation
  identity, output root, resource term, lifecycle term, registration identity,
  authority value, or canonical serialization differs
- **THEN** publication fails closed without substituting a nearby artifact,
  changing a seed or term, loading a dependency, creating the output root, or
  reusing the 20260808-r2 identity for altered content

#### Scenario: Registration review completes
- **WHEN** the canonical registration and review are committed and pushed
- **THEN** they grant eligibility only for a later separate exact execution-
  request proposal and grant no approval, authorization, native loading,
  environment construction, seed access, fitting, training, evaluation, OPE,
  gameplay, CommunicationMod, qualification, promotion, policy-quality, or
  causal authority

#### Scenario: Execution-chain content is proposed in this change
- **WHEN** an execution request, standing-delegation resolution, delegated
  approval, authorization, execution journal, checkpoint, or terminal experiment
  artifact is included in the 20260808-r2 registration publication scope
- **THEN** the change is invalid and SHALL stop before publication
