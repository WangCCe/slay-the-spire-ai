## Context

The r2 live context target contains 799 guard-replacement opportunities. Its
floor-28-34 stratum has 59 rows across 17 context cells and only five runs.
The closed aligned gate matched 35 rows (`0.593220338983051`) and missed six
cells carrying 24 rows. Three missing cells carrying 15 rows already occur in
the prior battle-index-10 fresh simulator corpus, but the supplemental fit
cohort sampled only battle index 3 and contributed no floor-28-34 target cell.

The target and prior fresh corpus have now informed cohort design. The prior
fresh metadata also exposed label-bearing fields during the attribution audit.
They are therefore development evidence, not untouched confirmation evidence.
The production r16 policy and CommunicationMod configuration remain outside
this experiment.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Collect enough fixed late-floor simulator rows to test the successor-delta
  representation with an actual paired fit.
- Preserve exact source, parent, native module, seed, corpus, target, and
  contamination provenance.
- Keep fresh policy labels inaccessible until both fitted arms and calibration
  thresholds are frozen.
- End with a hard decision: propose an independent live confirmation change or
  close this successor recipe.

**Non-Goals:**

- Reinterpreting the failed `0.593220` result as a pass.
- Reusing any previous fresh policy labels as confirmation evidence.
- Changing context cells, support thresholds, labels, optimizer, update count,
  calibration rule, or policy gates.
- Gameplay, CommunicationMod, online learning, hyperparameter search,
  qualification, promotion, or production checkpoint writes.

## Decisions

### Use fixed battle-index-10 cohorts

Register fit seeds `288000..290047` (2,048 profiles), calibration seeds
`290048..290559` (512 profiles), and fresh seeds `291000..292023` (1,024
profiles), all at battle index 10 with the existing maximum of two retained
source states per profile. The ranges are disjoint from the action-relative
successor lineage inspected before proposal creation.

The existing battle-index-10 sample produced the reachable missing cells with
only 1,536 profiles, so 2,048 fixed fit profiles provide a bounded opportunity
to repair train support without stopping when a desired cell appears.

Alternative: collect only until coverage exceeds `0.60`. Rejected because the
target-dependent stopping rule would make corpus size and evidence adaptive.

Alternative: include battle index 3 again. Rejected because its prior train
supplement added 712 rows but zero floor-28-34 target cells.

### Separate collection from conditional fitting

The first committed registration binds the collector, native module, frozen
r16 parent, items export, previous merged fit/calibration inputs, live
development target, exact cohorts, output limits, and authority. It publishes
new partition corpora, deterministic merges, a context-only fresh projection,
support evidence, and a manifest.

After collection is sealed, a second committed registration binds every
resulting hash and the unchanged paired recipe. The fit runner evaluates the
registered support once, constructs no optimizer on failure, and otherwise
fits the two arms once.

Alternative: collect and fit in one process. Rejected because a sealed corpus
boundary lets the fit registration bind generated bytes without repeating
collection after a fit-side failure.

### Publish a fresh context projection before labels are accessible

Collection publishes the new fresh partition's context cell IDs and the four
support covariates in a canonical context-only artifact. The conditional fit
uses that projection for the pre-optimizer support gate and verifies it against
the sealed fresh corpus hash. It does not deserialize the label-bearing fresh
corpus until both arms and thresholds are frozen.

Alternative: load the full fresh corpus and promise not to read label fields.
Rejected because the serialized object co-locates context and policy labels and
cannot provide an auditable access boundary.

### Treat existing target support as development evidence

The existing 799-row target remains immutable and supplies context weights,
but the report labels every support and model result `development_only=true`.
The leave-one-late-run-out coverage range (`0.388889..0.813953`) is reported as
a clustering warning, not used to alter the fixed gate.

A hard-policy pass or descriptive paired-control pass permits only a new
proposal with an independent real-game target and a minimum late-run condition.
It grants no direct LightSTS gate, live candidate evaluation, or promotion.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Rare cells may remain absent after 2,048 fit profiles] -> Keep the cohort
  fixed and close the recipe rather than extend or search seeds.
- [One newly covered row can cross the old `0.60` gate] -> Report exact cells,
  run-cluster sensitivity, and development-only authority; require independent
  live confirmation before any policy claim.
- [Fresh labels could leak through shared serialization] -> Publish and use a
  context-only projection until both arms and thresholds are frozen.
- [A new fit may consume time without improving policy metrics] -> Use one
  fixed 4,096-update execution and close on neither hard nor descriptive pass.
- [Native module bytes may drift] -> Use a new immutable run-scoped build path
  if rebuilding is necessary and bind its exact hash before collection.

## Migration Plan

1. Implement and regression-test registration, collection, deterministic merge,
   context projection, support, and deferred-fit boundaries.
2. Run focused tests, strict OpenSpec validation, and one timed commit gate;
   commit and push source plus collection registration.
3. Execute the registered fixed collection once and seal its artifacts.
4. Commit the exact fit registration, then evaluate support and conditionally
   run the paired fit once.
5. Commit terminal reports, sync specs, and archive the change.

Rollback is deletion of the additive development artifacts. Existing corpora,
target, parent checkpoint, gameplay configuration, and production policy are
not modified.
